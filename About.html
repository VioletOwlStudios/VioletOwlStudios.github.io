ABOUT US

What is Violet Owl Studios:

Violet Owl Studios is a Solo ran studio created by EnderGames. Started in 2023 under the name StarVoid Studios, Ender set to make this studio a place where he could escape to share his creativity to the world.


Who is EnderGames:

EnderGames is a 25 year old Indie Game Dev and Founder of Violet Owl Studios. Graduate of Full Sail University with a Bachelor Degree in Game Design and experince in Unreal Engine. Ender is also an inspiring Graphics Designer and Digital Cinematographer.


Why Violet Owl Studios?

The Name Violet Owl comes from Enders faveroit color and the noctoural nature of the owl. as to why this place was created, mainly as a spot to post his work thats not just a regular portfolio site
